Refund policy

This site takes no payment, so there is nothing here to refund. If you bought a testing package, the refund terms that apply are the ones attached to that purchase.

1. This site does not sell anything

Reading this guide costs nothing and involves no transaction. No payment is taken here, so no refund can be issued here.

2. Which policy applies to a purchase

If you bought a closed testing package, you purchased it from the provider on their own checkout. Their refund terms govern that transaction, and they are shown at the point of sale. Read them before you pay, especially the part covering what happens if a test window fails.

3. What is usually reasonable to expect

Refund terms vary between providers, but these are the situations worth checking for in any agreement before you buy:

  • The window never started. You paid, but testers were never opted in and no qualifying period began.
  • The window broke. The opted-in count fell below the required minimum during the period, so the fortnight restarted.
  • Testers were not real. Activity came from emulators, scripted installs, or accounts created for the campaign.
  • A re-test instead of a refund. Some providers offer to run the window again at no cost rather than returning the money. Both are reasonable, but you should know which you are agreeing to.

4. What a refund cannot cover

A refund of a testing package does not cover Google's developer registration fee, which is paid to Google and is not something a testing provider can return. It also cannot be conditioned on an approval outcome, because no provider controls how Google reviews an application.

5. Be wary of the refund guarantee

A promise to refund in every circumstance sounds reassuring and often is not. What matters is whether the condition is stated precisely: refunded if what, measured how, within what period. Vague guarantees are difficult to claim against. Precise ones are easy to hold a provider to.

6. How to raise a problem

Raise it with the provider you paid, using the support channel on the page you ordered from, and do it while the window is still live rather than after it closes. Support during an active campaign is far more useful than a dispute after one.

7. If you have not purchased yet

Nothing here requires you to. Running the closed test yourself costs nothing beyond your time, and the guides cover the whole process. The paid option exists for developers who cannot hold twelve testers opted in for a fortnight.
