How to add testers in Play Console

Adding a name is the easy part. The step that decides whether your count moves is what the tester does afterwards, and it is where most closed tests silently stall.

Get started Full walkthrough

Two ways to add people

Play Console accepts either individual Google addresses or a Google Group. Both work; they fail differently.

Email list

Fastest for a dozen people you already know. Every address has to be added individually, and each tester must open the opt-in link signed in to that exact account.

Google Group

Better for larger or changing groups. You add the group once, and members are managed from the group itself. Members still have to opt in individually.

The step that gets missed

Being on the list is not the same as being opted in. For a tester to count, they have to:

  1. Be signed in to the Google account that appears on your tester list.
  2. Open the opt-in link from the closed testing track.
  3. Accept the invitation to become a tester.
  4. Install the app from the Play Store listing that opens.

Miss any of those and the tester shows up as invited but not opted in, and your counter does not move.

Write the instructions down for them

Send testers the opt-in link plus these four steps in plain text. The single most common cause of a stalled test is a tester who opened the link while signed in to a personal account they do not normally use on that phone.

Fixing a count that will not move

  • Testers cannot see the app at allThey are signed in to a different Google account than the one you listed. Ask them to check which account is active in the Play Store app.
  • They installed but the count stayed putThe install did not come from the Play listing. An APK you sent directly will not register as a tester install.
  • The opt-in link leads nowhereThe track is not published yet. Publishing is what makes the link live.
  • The link works but the app is not foundThe tester account was added to a different track than the one you published the release on.

How many to add

Add more than twelve. The requirement counts opted-in testers at the lowest point of the window, so spare names are not padding, they are the thing that absorbs a drop-out without your window restarting. Two or three spares is a reasonable buffer for a small test.

Removing testers

You can remove addresses from the list, but think about when. Removing people while the window is running is the fastest way to push your opted-in count under twelve. If someone has already gone inactive, replacing them is usually safer than pruning.

Never share credentials

Nobody needs your Play Console password, your signing key, or access to your developer account to test your app. Testers need one thing: the opt-in link. Anyone asking for more is asking for more than the job requires.
