Contact

Most closed testing questions have a written answer already. If yours does not, here is where to take it and what to include so you get a useful reply the first time.

Check these first

Four problems account for most questions sent in. Each has a fix written out already.

  • The tester count will not moveHow to add testers in Play Console covers the opt-in step testers keep missing.
  • The 14 days did not countThe timeline post explains what restarts the window.
  • Not sure which track to useInternal, closed, or open sets out which one the rule counts.
  • Production access was refusedRejected apps is the place to start.

During a live test window

If your 14 days are already running, do not wait on an email. Support for an active campaign is handled through the service that is running it, and the checkout page you ordered from carries the live support channel. That is deliberate: the problems that cost you a day are the ones that need an answer in minutes, not overnight.

What to include when you ask

A useful reply needs four things. Paste them in, and skip the back-and-forth.

  • Which track you are running, and whether the release is published.
  • The date your first twelve testers were opted in.
  • What the opted-in count says today.
  • What you have already tried.

Reporting a correction

If something on this site contradicts Google's published policy, that is worth fixing. Include the page and the sentence, and a link to the policy wording you are comparing it against. Corrections that come with a source get applied fastest.

Policy questions

Questions about how Google will treat your specific account, app, or application are answered by Google, not by this site. The policy pages in Play Console Help and the support options inside Play Console are the routes that can actually give you a definitive answer.

Never send credentials

No support route, here or anywhere else, needs your Play Console password, your signing key, or your keystore. If someone asks for those to help with a closed test, stop and use a different route.
