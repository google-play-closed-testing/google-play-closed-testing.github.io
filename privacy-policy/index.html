Privacy policy

This is a static informational site. It does not have accounts, does not set its own cookies, and does not ask you for personal information.

What this site collects directly

Nothing. There is no sign-up, no login, no contact form, no newsletter, and no comment system. No personal information is requested from you anywhere on this site, and none is stored by it.

Cookies and tracking

This site sets no cookies of its own and runs no analytics, advertising, or tracking scripts. There is no pixel, no session recording, and no profiling.

Third-party resources every page loads

Pages here load three external resources so the site renders correctly. Loading them means your browser makes a request to those providers, which will see your IP address and user agent as part of serving the file:

  • Google Fonts for the display and body typefaces.
  • unpkg for the Phosphor icon font.
  • testerbee.com only when you click a start button, which opens their page.

Each of those providers handles that request under its own privacy policy. This site does not receive or store any of it.

Hosting logs

Like any website, this one is served by a hosting provider that may keep standard access logs, such as the pages requested and the requesting IP address. Those logs are kept for operating and securing the service, and this site does not use them to identify anyone.

Links to other sites

Start buttons and some references link to third-party sites, including the checkout page for the managed testing service and Google's own policy documentation. Once you follow one of those links you are on that site, and its privacy policy applies rather than this one.

What this site never asks for

No part of this site, and no legitimate support route connected to it, needs your Play Console password, your keystore, your signing key, or your Google account credentials. If anyone asks you for those while claiming to help with a closed test, do not provide them.

Children

This site is written for app developers and is not directed at children. No personal information is knowingly collected from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If the site's behaviour changes, for example by adding analytics or a contact form, this page will be updated to describe it before that change goes live.

Questions

Questions about this policy can be raised through the routes on the contact page.
