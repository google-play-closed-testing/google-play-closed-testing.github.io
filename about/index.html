About this guide

A reference for one specific problem: getting an Android app through Google Play's closed testing requirement without wasting a fortnight on a test that does not count.

What this site is

A written guide to Google Play closed testing. It explains the requirement, walks through the setup, and covers the failure modes that make a 14-day window restart. It also links to a managed service for developers who would rather not recruit and hold twelve testers themselves.

What this site is not

It is not affiliated with Google, and it does not speak for Google. Every claim about the rule links back to Google's own documentation so you can verify it against your console. Where a claim cannot be verified that way, it is framed as advice rather than as policy.

The rules this site holds itself to

  • Source links on policy claims. If it is about what Google requires, there is a link to Google's documentation next to it.
  • No borrowed numbers. Review counts, approval rates, and app totals belong to the business that earned them. This site does not republish another company's figures as its own.
  • No guaranteed outcomes. Nobody controls a Google review. Anyone promising a guaranteed approval is selling something they cannot deliver.
  • Plain language. If a sentence is harder to read than the policy it describes, it has failed.

Check your own console

Requirements change, and they can differ by account type and creation date. The requirement shown in your Play Console is the one that applies to you. Treat any article, including everything here, as a starting point rather than as the final word.

How the site is organised

  • Guides are the procedures: how to set the track up, how to add testers, how to apply.
  • Blog posts each answer one question that comes up repeatedly.
  • Services covers what a managed test should contain, and Pricing explains what changes the cost.
  • Rejected apps is the starting point if a decision has already gone against you.

Questions or corrections

If something here is out of step with the policy, that is worth fixing. The contact page explains how to raise it.
